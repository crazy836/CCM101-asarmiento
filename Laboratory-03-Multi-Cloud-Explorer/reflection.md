# Mission  Reflection

After doing Mission 3, I learned more about AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. The cloud platform that impressed me the most is Google Cloud. I chose GCP because I found its AI, machine learning, and Kubernetes services interesting. Since I am an IT student, I think learning about these technologies can help me in the future, especially if I work with modern applications.

I noticed that AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All of them provide services for virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, and application development. They also allow companies to increase or decrease their resources depending on their needs. The main difference I noticed is that they have different service names and each provider has areas where they are stronger.

Business requirements are also important when choosing a cloud provider. For example, if a company already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory, I think Azure would be a good choice because it works well with Microsoft products. For a company focused on AI and machine learning, I would consider GCP. For a company that needs many different cloud services and scalability, AWS can also be a good option.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that I should not just choose a cloud provider because it is popular. I need to understand what the client needs first, such as their budget, application, users, security, and future growth.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after this mission. I added research about the three cloud providers, comparison tables, client recommendations, the decision matrix, Linux investigation, screenshots, and my reflection. I also became more familiar with writing Markdown and organizing my work in GitHub. Overall, this mission helped me understand cloud computing in a more practical way.
